# MedVoice Companion - Demo Video Script

> **Duration:** 2 minutes maximum  
> **Goal:** Show impact early, demonstrate Gemini 3 capabilities

---

## 🎬 Scene Breakdown

### Opening Hook (0:00 - 0:15)
**[Show confused elderly person looking at medical report]**

> *"My grandmother just got her lab results. 47 pages of numbers she doesn't understand. She's worried, but she can't reach her doctor until Monday."*

> *"What if she could just... ask her laptop? or mobile phone?"*

**[Transition to MedVoice Companion app]**

---

### Demo: Core Feature (0:15 - 0:45)
**[Screen recording of app]**

1. **Upload Document**
   - Show dragging a lab report image to the app
   - "Simply upload any medical document..."

2. **AI Analysis in Action**
   - Show loading animation with Gemini processing
   - "Gemini 3's multimodal AI analyzes the document..."

3. **Results Displayed**
   - Show simple summary, lab values with color coding
   - "...and translates it into plain English."

---

### Demo: Voice Feature (0:45 - 1:15)
**[Continue screen recording]**

4. **Voice Explanation**
   - Click "Listen" button, show audio playing
   - "And for users who prefer to listen, or can't see the screen well..."
   - *[Play brief audio clip of explanation]*

5. **Voice Q&A**
   - Tap microphone, ask "Is my cholesterol okay?"
   - Show AI response with context-aware answer
   - "They can ask follow-up questions, just by speaking."

---

### Accessibility Showcase (1:15 - 1:30)
**[Quick montage]**

- Toggle high contrast mode
- Toggle large text
- Switch language to Spanish
- Show multi-language explanation

> *"Built for everyone—young or old, any language, any ability."*

---

### Impact & Close (1:30 - 2:00)
**[Return to grandmother scenario]**

> *"With MedVoice Companion, my grandmother doesn't have to wait until Monday. She doesn't have to worry alone."*

> *"She just asks, and she understands."*

**[Show app logo]**

> *"MedVoice Companion. Your AI Health Translator."*

**[End with Gemini 3 badge]**

---

## 📋 Recording Checklist

- [ ] Clear screen recording (1080p minimum)
- [ ] Good audio quality
- [ ] Show real Gemini API responses (not mocked)
- [ ] Include captions/subtitles
- [ ] Background music (royalty-free)
- [ ] End card with app link
