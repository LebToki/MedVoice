## Project Description (for Kaggle - keep under 2000 words)

### The Problem

Millions of patients receive medical reports they can't understand. Complex terminology, confusing lab values, and dense prescriptions create anxiety and lead to medication non-compliance. This is especially challenging for elderly users and those with visual impairments.

### The Solution

**MedVoice Companion** uses Gemini 3 Pro's multimodal AI to transform any medical document into simple, spoken explanations.

### Key Features

- **📸 Document Analysis** - Upload PDFs or photos of medical reports, lab results, prescriptions
- **🗣️ Voice Explanations** - Hear your results explained in plain language
- **🧪 Lab Value Decoder** - Understand what your blood tests actually mean
- **💊 Medication Guide** - Learn about prescriptions and potential interactions
- **🎙️ Voice Q&A** - Ask follow-up questions hands-free
- **♿ Full Accessibility** - High contrast, large text, multi-language support

### Gemini 3 Pro Capabilities Used

1. **Multimodal Vision** - Analyzes images of medical documents
2. **Advanced Reasoning** - Interprets complex medical information accurately
3. **Natural Language Generation** - Creates simple, patient-friendly explanations
4. **Context-Aware Q&A** - Answers follow-up questions with full context

### Impact

MedVoice Companion empowers patients to understand their health, reducing anxiety and improving healthcare outcomes. It bridges the gap between medical professionals and patients who deserve to understand their own bodies.
